Privacy Policy

Not My Tempo · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Not My Tempo does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example, how many devices use it) and crash reports, which you can turn off. Anything else leaves your device only when you choose to send us feedback, or when you buy Pro through Apple.

Microphone & speech

The app uses your device's microphone for three things: recognizing voice commands, detecting the tempo of nearby music, and the chromatic tuner.

  • Speech recognition runs on-device (requiresOnDeviceRecognition). Audio is processed locally and is not sent to Apple or to us.
  • Audio is analyzed in real time and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn how many people use the app and fix crashes, Not My Tempo sends anonymous statistics to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), read only by the developer:

  • a random ID created when the app is installed (not linked to you, your Apple ID, or your device; it changes if you reinstall)
  • app version, iOS version, device type, and region/language setting
  • how many times the app was opened, the install date, and the days it was opened
  • steps in the Pro purchase screen (shown, started, completed), never payment details
  • crash and freeze reports

No audio, settings, or personal information is included. Statistics are sent only if you are signed in to iCloud. You can turn them off at any time under Voice Commands → Privacy → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your last-used settings (tempo, time signature, subdivision, click volume, beat-view style, and practice-mode options), your accent presets, and a simple count of how often you open the app (used only to decide when to ask how you're enjoying it). All of this stays on your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you send feedback from inside the app, we receive what you write, the feedback type, your app version, device model and iOS version, and your region/language setting — plus your name and email only if you enter them so we can reply. It is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and read only by the developer to answer you and improve the app. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. You can also simply email us instead.

Purchases

Not My Tempo Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your account.

Network

The metronome, voice control, and tuner work fully offline. The app only goes online to send feedback you chose to send, to check for replies to it, and to talk to the App Store for purchases. It also sends the anonymous statistics described above, unless you turn them off.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
